Introduction

Welcome to Stenoip Company. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information across our suite of products and services, including Oodles, which is our metasearch engine; Ringzauber, which is our web browser; and Praterich, which is our AI application. By accessing or using any Stenoip Company products, you agree to the data practices outlined in this policy. Because our services are designed for general audiences of all ages, we take extra care to ensure our practices remain transparent for all users.

Information We Do Not Collect

To protect your day-to-day digital footprint, Stenoip Company has structured its core products to operate without intrusive tracking. Currently, we do not require you to register, sign up, or create a user account to use Oodles, Ringzauber, or Praterich. Furthermore, Oodles does not store your search histories or track personal identifiers. Similarly, Ringzauber currently operates without collecting or cloud-syncing your browsing history, bookmarks, diagnostic logs, or telemetry data.

Product-Specific Data Practices and Third-Party AI Routing

When you use Praterich, your prompts and inputs are processed depending on the model you select. If you choose to use the Google Gemini model option, your prompts, inputs, and related data are transmitted directly to Google via Google AI Studio for processing, and Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's respective privacy policies. If you choose the alternative Qwen model option processed via Groq infrastructure, your data routing adheres to minimal retention and processing standards designed to protect your interaction content.

Regarding upcoming features, Ringzauber will introduce an optional file-syncing feature in a future holiday update around Christmas, and when launched, this policy will be updated to reflect any minimal data required to securely sync your files across your personal devices.

International Users and Regional Privacy Rights

Because Stenoip Company products are accessible globally, we acknowledge international privacy standards, including regulations originating from regions outside the United States such as the European Union's GDPR and domestic state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. Although we collect minimal to no personal information due to our lack of user accounts, if you have questions regarding your data or wish to exercise rights under applicable local privacy frameworks, you may contact us directly at customerserviceforstenoip@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Stenoip Company products are built for general use by everyone. Because we do not require account creation, track personal identifiers, or collect behavioral profiles, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or 16 where applicable. If you believe a child has provided unauthorized data through a third-party integration, please reach out to us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect new product features such as the upcoming Ringzauber file-sync update or shifting technical integrations. When we make updates, we will post a prominent notice directly on our official websites. Your continued use of our products after modifications are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us via email at customerserviceforstenoip@gmail.com or by mail at our location in New York State, USA.
