Ringzauber Python will be retired this late 2026

Written by Humans

Ringzauber was released on the 13th of September 2025 by Stenoip Company. Development has spanned for 2 years using PyQt as the base for the browser, A.I, VPN and more. However, PyQt began to show vulnerabilities in production.

It is ancient

Stenoip Company attempted to fix the ongoing problem of HTML videos not appearing. The problem could never be sourced up to the writing of the article. Ringzauber Py also used alot of tested computer's memory, decreasing the speed of user multitasking.

Difficult to Update

Since 2021, Stenoip's developers has been experienced in the field of web technologies like HTML, CSS and Javascript. However, the differences in format and structure between HTML and PyQt was wide. Due to this, Stenoip Company released updates for the simple features like "allowing links to open in a new tab" and "allowing camera access" than developing new solutions like A.I agents.

The successor

Ringzauber's successor will be Ringzauber JS*. Unlike the Python Browser, rJS will be made from Electron.js. Updates for privacy, innovation and the future will be frequent and the browser will be up to par with modern browsers

*Name may be subject to change
